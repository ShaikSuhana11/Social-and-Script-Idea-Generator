# Brand Knowledge Base - Social Media Content Guidelines

## Brand Voice
- Tone: Professional yet conversational, friendly, and approachable
- Personality: Innovative, trustworthy, and customer-centric
- Avoid: Slang overload, aggressive sales pitches, controversial topics

## Platform-Specific Guidelines

### Instagram
- Optimal caption length: 125-150 characters before "more" cutoff
- Use 3-5 relevant hashtags per post
- Emojis: 2-3 per caption for engagement
- Include clear call-to-action (CTA)

### Twitter/X
- Character limit: 280 characters
- Use 1-2 hashtags maximum
- Threads for longer content
- Engage with trends and current events (relevant ones only)

### LinkedIn
- Professional tone, longer form content (1300-2000 characters)
- Industry insights and thought leadership
- Minimal emojis (1-2 max)
- No hashtag spam (3-5 max)

### Facebook
- Conversational tone
- 80-100 characters for optimal engagement on short posts
- Can be longer for storytelling
- Include images or videos for higher reach

## Content Pillars
1. Product Education - Feature highlights, tutorials
2. Customer Success - Testimonials, case studies
3. Industry Insights - Trends, news, analysis
4. Behind-the-Scenes - Team, culture, process
5. Community Engagement - UGC, polls, questions

## Best Practices
- Always include a CTA
- Use active voice
- Avoid jargon when possible
- Include relevant emojis sparingly
- Tag relevant accounts/partners
- Use line breaks for readability
- Proofread before posting
- Post during peak hours (9-11 AM, 1-3 PM local time)

## Hashtag Strategy
- Mix of broad, niche, and branded hashtags
- Research trending tags before using
- Create branded hashtags for campaigns

## Compliance
- Disclose sponsored content (#ad, #sponsored)
- Obtain permission before using UGC
- Follow platform TOS and FTC guidelines
- Avoid making unverifiable claims
